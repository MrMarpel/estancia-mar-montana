Location

In the heart of the
Port of Jávea

The apartment is located in the Port of Jávea (Xàbia), less than 5 minutes' walk from Playa de la Grava and with the Montgó Natural Reserve just around the corner. A quiet residential area surrounded by supermarkets, bakeries, pharmacies and charming Mediterranean restaurants.

Distances

  • Playa de la Grava 350 m · 4–5 min walk
  • Playa de la Montañar 1.2 km · 12–15 min walk
  • Playa del Arenal 2.8 km · 5–7 min by car
  • Montgó Natural Reserve 250 m
  • Restaurants (La Cocina, Sabor a Mi…) 50 m
  • La Sella Golf 11 km
  • Alicante or Valencia Airport 100 km

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know
before booking in Jávea

How far is the beach?

Playa de la Grava is just 350 metres away — about 4–5 minutes on foot. Playa de la Montañar is 1.2 km away (12–15 min walk) and Playa del Arenal is 2.8 km away (5–7 min by car).

Is there free parking?

Yes, there is free public parking in the vicinity. There is also an electric vehicle charging point 100 metres away.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, small pets are welcome upon prior request and with respect for the space. A small supplement may apply. Please enquire when booking.

How many guests can stay?

The apartment accommodates up to 4 guests: a master bedroom with a double bed and a second bedroom with two single beds.

How much does it cost per night?

Rates vary by season:

  • Low season (Oct–Mar): from €90/night
  • Mid season (Apr–Jun, Sep): from €130/night
  • High season (Jul–Aug): from €200/night

All prices include taxes.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in: 12:00 to 18:00 — please notify your arrival time in advance.
Check-out: by 12:00.

Does it have air conditioning?

Yes, there is hot/cold air conditioning in all rooms, plus a ceiling fan in the master bedroom.

Is it suitable for families with children?

Absolutely. The apartment is suitable for children of all ages. A free cot can be requested for babies aged 0–3 years. It is also wheelchair accessible.

What is nearby?

Restaurants 50 metres away, supermarkets and pharmacies nearby, the Montgó Natural Reserve 250 m away and Jávea's historic town centre just a few kilometres away. The port has everything you need for a car-free holiday.

How can I book?

You can book via Airbnb with secure payment and full guarantee, or contact Xusa directly via WhatsApp (+34 697 359 158) for an immediate response and direct booking.
